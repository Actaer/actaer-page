# Why We Started Actaer

Actaer is a software product company from Novi Pazar, Serbia, founded in 2025. We build the tools we wished existed — and we help companies put AI to work. This is the short version of why.

*Written by the Actaer team — the engineers behind VantumIQP and FaberPDF.*

## The problem we couldn't unsee

Somewhere along the way, software stopped being on your side.

You know the pattern. The BI platform that needs a full-time babysitter before it shows you a single trustworthy number. The PDF editor that won't let you sign a contract until you upload it to someone else's cloud and start a subscription. The consultants who study your business for three months, hand you a deck, and leave before anything runs.

We spent years inside that pattern — as engineers, as users, as the people called in to clean up afterwards. At some point the frustration turned into a question we couldn't put down: what would these tools look like if someone built them to serve the person using them?

## So we built our own

We started with the two problems that annoyed us most.

The first was business intelligence. Most companies have plenty of data and very few answers. Dashboards drift, numbers disagree, and nobody can say where a chart came from. [VantumIQP](/products/vantumiqp) is our answer: a governed BI workspace built on Apache Superset. Ask a question, explore it in SQL, and publish a dashboard your whole company can trust — every chart traceable to its query, with access control your auditors will actually like. It's for teams that need reporting both executives and regulators believe.

The second was documents. Signing or fixing a PDF should not require an account, a subscription, and a trip through a stranger's server. [FaberPDF](/products/faberpdf) is a local-first PDF editor for Windows and Linux. Sign, annotate, edit text, and validate documents right on your desktop — your files never leave your machine. It's free during beta, and it's for anyone whose documents are their business: legal teams, accountants, operations, you.

## And we consult — differently

We still consult. But we do it the way we'd want to be consulted.

[AI Consulting](/consulting/ai-consulting) is our flagship service. We find where AI actually pays off in your business, build the automation, and train your people to own it. You don't get a strategy deck. You get working systems and a team that knows how to keep them working.

Building products is what keeps us honest. We live with our own architecture decisions, support our own users, and fix our own bugs. That discipline follows us onto your project — because we can't recommend practices on Monday that we'd never accept in our own codebase on Tuesday.

## What you can expect from us

We're small, senior, and deliberate. That's not a limitation — it's the product. You talk to the people who do the work. You get plain answers, even when they're not the easy ones. And you get software we stand behind, because our name is on it.

On this blog, expect the same: honest writing about building products, putting AI to work, and the engineering trade-offs behind both.

If something in your company is slower or harder than it should be, tell us about it. [Reach out](/contact) — we'll give you a straight answer about whether we can help.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does Actaer build?**

Two products: [VantumIQP](/products/vantumiqp), a governed business intelligence workspace built on Apache Superset, and [FaberPDF](/products/faberpdf), a local-first PDF editor for Windows and Linux, currently in beta.

**Does Actaer still offer consulting?**

Yes. [AI Consulting](/consulting/ai-consulting) is our flagship service — we find where AI pays off, build the automation, and train your team to own it. We also take on software development and digital modernization work.

**Where is Actaer based?**

Novi Pazar, Serbia. We founded the company in 2025.
